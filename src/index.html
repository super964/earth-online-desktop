Welcome to Earth Online

A living digital twin of the planet. Real seismic sensors, weather services and satellites feed this globe every minute. Your job: observe, probe, and log the planet's pulse.

Click anywhere on the globe to pull live weather, local time and nearby hazards for that coordinate.

02Read the stream

The right panel lists warnings, quakes, storms and fires as they arrive. Click an entry to fly to it.

03Travel in time

Rewind up to 30 days to replay the planet's seismic history and see the sun where it was.
